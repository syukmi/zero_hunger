Male Reproductive Anatomy

Overview of the Penis

The penis is an external organ of the male reproductive and urinary systems. Its main functions include excretion of urine and sexual reproduction.

Anatomical Structure

  • Root: The internal attachment point connecting to the pelvic wall.
  • Body (Shaft): The main external portion containing erectile tissue (corpora cavernosa and corpus spongiosum).
  • Glans: The sensitive tip or head of the organ.

Key Functions & Health

Maintaining proper hygiene and regular health check-ups is essential for preventing infections and addressing issues like erectile dysfunction or STI prevention.
